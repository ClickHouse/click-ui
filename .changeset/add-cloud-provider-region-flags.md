Add 16 flags so the set covers every country with a cloud provider region: Austria, Bahrain, Belgium, Chile, China, Denmark, Finland, Greece, Italy, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Spain, and Thailand. All are exported from the Figma library with the standardized rounded corners.

Also corrects nine existing flags that had drifted from their official artwork. Canada's maple leaf was horizontally squashed and is now correctly proportioned. Canada and Switzerland had their reds transposed, so Canada is now `#d52b1e` and Switzerland `#ff0000`. The red band on the United Arab Emirates flag was too narrow and is now the correct width. Colors are corrected on Indonesia (`#ff0000` to `#e70011`), Singapore (`#ed2939` to `#df0000`), Ireland (`#169b62` to `#009a49`, `#ff883e` to `#ff7900`), Sweden (`#006aa7` to `#005293`, `#fecc00` to `#fecb00`), France (`#002395` to `#000091`, `#ed2939` to `#e1000f`) and Taiwan (`#000094` to `#000095`, `#fe0000` to `#ff0000`).
